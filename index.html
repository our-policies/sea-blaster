Sea Blaster: Pirate Ships

Arcade pirate shooter · Nadesoft

Steer your pirate ship with one finger while your cannons aim and fire on their own. Sink enemy fleets, chain kills into combos, grab power-ups and take on a boss every five stages. Spend the gold you win on upgrades and new ships, then sail further on your next run.

Features

  • Simple one-finger controls with auto-aiming cannons
  • Five seas to explore, from tropical bays to haunted isles
  • Boss battles, combos, power-ups and daily missions
  • Seven pirate ships to unlock, each with its own weapons
  • No ads, no account and no personal data collected
  • Plays completely offline
