Privacy Policy

Sea Blaster: Pirate Ships · Nadesoft · Effective date: 20 September 2026
In short: Sea Blaster: Pirate Ships does not collect, share or sell any personal information. Your game progress stays on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android game Sea Blaster: Pirate Ships (package name com.nadesoft.seablaster, the "Game") handles information. The Game is published by Nadesoft ("we", "us").

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The Game does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

2. Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the Game saves the following information locally on your device only:

  • your coins, best stage reached, number of ships sunk and number of runs;
  • the upgrade levels you bought in the shop;
  • your sound and music settings.

This data is never sent to us or to any third party. It is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the Game.

3. Advertising, analytics and tracking

The Game contains no advertisements, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no tracking technologies. We do not use advertising identifiers.

4. Permissions

The Game does not request any special permissions such as camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage access.

5. Third-party services

The Game is built with the Unity engine. The Game does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics or social-media SDKs. If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before the change is released.

6. In-app purchases

The Game currently has no real-money purchases. Coins are earned by playing and can only be spent on in-game upgrades.

7. Children's privacy

Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Game contains cartoon-style pirate ship combat and no user-generated content or online interaction.

8. Data security

Since your progress is stored only on your device, its security depends on your device's own protections. We do not operate any servers that store data about you.

9. Your rights and choices

You can delete all data the Game stores by clearing the app's data in your Android settings (Settings → Apps → Sea Blaster: Pirate Ships → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the Game. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing further for us to access, correct or delete.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at the same location and the "Effective date" above will be updated.

11. us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, Nadesoft at: contact@nadesoft.bd
