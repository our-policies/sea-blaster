Sea Blaster: Pirate Ships · Nadesoft
Need help? Email us at contact@nadesoft.bd. Please include your phone model, whether you play on Android or iOS, and the game version shown at the bottom of Settings. A screenshot helps a lot.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Drag left and right to steer your ship. Your cannons aim and fire on their own; dodge enemy shots and rocks, and collect coins and power-ups. Every fifth stage ends with a boss. You can replay the tutorial at any time from Settings → Tutorial → Replay.

Where is my progress saved?

Your coins, upgrades, ships and achievements are saved on your device after every run. On Android you can also sign in with Google Play Games in Settings to back up your progress and restore it on a new device. The iOS version keeps progress on the device only, so deleting the app deletes your progress.

I lost my progress on a new Android phone

Open Settings and sign in with the same Google Play Games account you used before. Your saved game is restored automatically after signing in.

The game runs slowly or gets hot

The game picks a quality level for your phone automatically. Close other apps running in the background and let the phone cool down if it is warm. You can turn on Show FPS in Settings to see the frame rate and include it when you contact us.

There is no sound

Check that Sound and Music are on in Settings and that your phone is not muted. On iPhone, the ring/silent switch also mutes the game.

Does the game have ads or in-app purchases?

No. There are no ads and no real-money purchases. Coins are earned by playing.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The whole game works without an internet connection. On Android, leaderboards and cloud backup need a connection.

Delete your data

We do not collect or store any personal data on our side, so there is no account with us to delete. Everything the game keeps is on your device:

  • iPhone: delete the app (choose Delete App, not Offload App). This removes all of its data.
  • Android: go to Settings → Apps → Sea Blaster: Pirate Ships → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the game.
  • Google Play Games (Android, optional): your saved game, achievements and leaderboard entries are stored by Google. Remove them in the Google Play Games app under Settings → Delete Play Games account and data, or in your Google Account.

Details are in our Privacy Policy. If you have a question about your data, email contact@nadesoft.bd.
